### ***Holiday Workplace Planning***

![Holiday Planning Image, Courtesy of GoogleImages](/img/holiday-planning.jpg "Holiday Planning Image, Courtesy of GoogleImages")

**Employee appreciation and engagement**

The holidays are an opportunity to recognize employees and strengthen workplace relationships.

* Plan holiday celebrations, lunches, or employee appreciation events.
* Consider gift cards, bonuses, or holiday gifts.
* Recognize employees who have gone above and beyond during the year.
* Make sure recognition is inclusive and does not favor certain departments or shifts.
* Consider employees who may be experiencing financial or personal difficulties.
* Give managers guidance on showing their and the company’s appreciation to their teams.

**\*Consideration**:* *Not every employee celebrates the same holidays. Keep activities inclusive and voluntary where appropriate.*

**Holiday schedules and time off**

This is one of the biggest areas requiring advance planning.

* Communicate holiday schedules early.
* Establish deadlines for requesting vacation and PTO.
* Review staffing requirements for critical roles.
* Establish procedures for approving overlapping vacation requests.
* Clarify whether employees can carry over their unused PTO.
* Communicate expectations for employees who must work holidays.
* Establish procedures for unexpected absences and call-outs.

**\*Consideration**: Make sure holiday scheduling decisions are consistent and fair across all departments.*

### ***2026 Year-end Compliance Checklist***

**Employee records and employment policies**

* Review and update the employee handbook for 2027.
* Obtain employee acknowledgment of receipt of updated policies, preferably electronically with a retained confirmation.
* Review job descriptions and employee classifications (exempt vs. nonexempt).
* Audit personnel files for missing documentation.
* Review I-9 forms and ensure required retention periods are followed.
* Verify employee emergency contact information and addresses.
* Review PTO, vacation, sick leave, and if applicable, carryover balances.
* Review outstanding performance evaluations and disciplinary actions.
* Confirm that required federal and state workplace posters are current.

**Payroll and compensation**

* Review employee pay rates and confirm wage adjustments for minimum wage.
* Verify overtime calculations and employee FLSA classifications.
* Reconcile payroll records and year-to-date earnings.
* Review bonuses, commissions, gift cards, and other taxable compensation.
* Confirm federal and state tax withholding information.
* Prepare for W-2 and 1099 reporting.
* Review employee benefit deductions as well as employer contributions.

For 2026, the IRS has revised W-2 reporting requirements, including new reporting fields for certain tipped occupations. W-2s must be furnished to employees and filed with the Social Security Administration by February 1, 2027.

**Delaware-specific compliance**

* **Delaware Paid Family and Medical Leave**: Ensure that the company is properly registered, contributions are being made, and required quarterly wage and hours reporting is current. The program became fully effective January 1, 2026.
* **Unemployment insurance**: Reconcile quarterly reports and payments.
* **Workers' compensation**: Verify that coverage is current and appropriate for all employees.
* **Delaware withholding tax**: Reconcile payroll withholding and state reporting.
* **New hire reporting**: Confirm required reports have been submitted.

DE requires workers' compensation coverage for employers with one or more employees working in the state.

**Federal employment law compliance**

* Review FMLA eligibility and employee leave records.
* Review ADA accommodation requests and documentation.
* Confirm compliance with FLSA wage and hour requirements.
* Review harassment and discrimination policies.
* Ensure required workplace safety records are maintained.
* Review OSHA injury and illness logs and determine whether annual posting is required.
* Review applicable EEO-1 reporting requirements.

**Employee benefits and insurance**

* Review health, dental, and vision insurance renewals.
* Confirm benefit enrollment and employee elections for 2027.
* Review 401(k) contributions and required plan notices.
* Verify life and disability insurance coverage.
* Review COBRA administration and qualifying event procedures.
* Confirm required ACA reporting if applicable.

Important: Companies with an average of at least 50 full-time employees, including full-time equivalents, during 2026 generally qualify as Applicable Large Employers for ACA purposes in 2027. This is a separate calculation from FMLA.

**OSHA and workplace safety**

* Review workplace accidents and injury reports.
* Update OSHA 300 and 301 logs, if required.
* Complete the OSHA 300A annual summary, if required.
* Have an appropriate company executive certify the summary.
* Post the 300A from February 1 through April 30 in 2027.
* Review safety training and emergency procedures.

**Important deadlines approaching**

* December 31, 2026 - Complete year-end HR and payroll review
* January 31, 2027 - Generally, Q4 federal payroll tax filing
* February 1, 2027 - Furnish and file W-2s
* February 1, 2027 - Begin OSHA 300A posting, if applicable
* April 30, 2027 - End of OSHA 300A posting period
